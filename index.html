01 · What this means for families

Look beyond a single percentage.

Prospective families should be able to understand not only what children achieve at KCA, but the kind of school behind those outcomes: ambitious, inclusive and committed to continual improvement.

Our approach

We do not believe inclusion and high expectations are competing ideas. Our job is to know our pupils well, make excellent teaching accessible and help every child achieve as strongly as possible from their individual starting point.

How we read performance

08 · Understanding the data

Read the figures with confidence.

Statutory assessment language can be technical. These short explanations help families understand what the measures do — and do not — tell us.

Expected standard

The level pupils are expected to reach by the end of the relevant statutory assessment point.

Higher standard / greater depth

A higher level of attainment. At KS2, reading and mathematics use scaled test scores; writing uses teacher assessment.

Cohort size matters

When a group contains only a few pupils, one child's outcome can move the percentage substantially. We always show the number of pupils alongside filtered results.
